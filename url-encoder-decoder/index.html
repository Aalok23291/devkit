URL encoder and decoder

Make text safe for URLs, or read an encoded one.

How to use it

  1. Paste text or an encoded string.
  2. Click Encode or Decode.
  3. Copy the result.

Example

name=Ada Lovelace&city=São Paulo

Frequently asked questions

Why do URLs need encoding?

Characters such as spaces, &, ? and non-English letters have special meaning or are not allowed in URLs, so they are replaced with % codes.

Which characters stay unchanged?

Letters, digits and a few symbols such as - _ . ~ are left as they are.
