About

DevKit is a collection of simple, free tools for working with data: formatting JSON and XML, converting YAML and CSV, encoding Base64 and URLs, decoding JWTs and comparing text.

The goal is to make small everyday tasks quick. Every tool runs entirely in your browser, so what you paste stays on your device.

The site is supported by advertising. If you have a suggestion for a new tool, please get in touch.

DevKit runs in your browser. Your data is never uploaded.
